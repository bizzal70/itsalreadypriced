## Today's Field Note
Three operational threads worth your attention today, none of them price noise. Solana DEX Velocity halted trading over a bug just two months after relaunching as a rebuild of Drift Protocol, the same protocol North Korea drained back in April, so treat an open position there as frozen and suspect until they publish specifics. Liquity V2's ETH collateral branch dropped to roughly 149%, below the 150% Safety Mode threshold, which restricts withdrawals and new debt until collateralization recovers. And Trezor confirmed its Nov. 11 firmware blocks known spying implants but cannot undo a seed phrase already captured, with CryptoBilis-supplied devices bought from March 2026 onward still flagged for migration.

## Today's Move
- If you have funds or open positions on Velocity (Solana), stop interacting and wait for a root-cause post before assuming withdrawals are safe.
- Liquity V2 ETH-branch borrowers: add collateral or reduce debt now, since Safety Mode near 149% blocks new withdrawals and borrows until it clears 150%.
- Anyone holding a Trezor from a CryptoBilis-supplied batch (bought March 2026 or later): generate a fresh seed on a verified device and migrate funds, do not just update firmware.
- Confirm your Trezor is on the Nov. 11 firmware regardless of supplier, then verify the fingerprint against Trezor's published values.
- Watch Velocity's and Liquity's official channels today for the specifics, not secondhand threads.
