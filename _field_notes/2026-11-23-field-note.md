## Today's Field Note
Three concurrent incidents, all costing real coins. Chainflip disclosed a 122.9 BTC theft with total losses climbing to $12 million, and is now begging the attacker to return the balance for a 10% bounty by Monday. Separately, a roughly $2 million exploit on Fusionist's ACE bridge on BSC (Blockaid pegged early extraction near $1.04 million in USDT) turned into a $3.7 million free-for-all as copycats emptied it, with Fusionist urging an immediate pause on ACE transfers, trades, and bridging. And the MoldyRoost malvertising campaign (480+ ad domains across 47+ countries) can drain a vulnerable iPhone's wallet recovery phrase with a single tap, so this is not a desktop-only threat.

## Today's Move
- If you hold or LP on Chainflip, stop routing swaps through it until the bounty deadline resolves and the drained funds are accounted for.
- Pause all ACE activity on BSC now (transfers, trades, bridging) per Fusionist, and revoke any outstanding approvals tied to the ACE bridge contract.
- Update your iPhone to the latest iOS immediately and never type or photograph a seed phrase on a phone; MoldyRoost needs only one tap on a malicious ad.
- Blur users: note 545.6 ETH across 315 contracts now lacks a rescue path after upgrades were disabled, so pull any idle ETH sitting in affected Blur contracts via normal withdrawal.
- Watch the Chainflip attacker address and the ACE drainer wallets for movement to mixers or CEX deposits before filing any recovery claims.
