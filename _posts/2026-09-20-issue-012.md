*Issue #012 · Week of September 20, 2026*

## This Week's Verdict

The exploits worth reading about this week did not touch a smart contract; they touched your laptop, your phone, and your npm lockfile. **North Korea** ran a device-level franchise, **Iran** ran a toll booth in the Strait of Hormuz, and Bitcoin ripped 24.6% on short liquidations while everyone congratulated themselves for calling it. As always, the money moved before the headline did.

## The Breaches

The number of the week comes from a joint law enforcement advisory on **WaterPlum**, the **North Korean** crew that compromised at least 30,000 devices worldwide between December 2025 and July 2026 and moved more than **$10.7 million** in stolen crypto back to Pyongyang. This is not a clever oracle manipulation. It is patient, industrial endpoint compromise at scale, and it worked.

Infrastructure took the second hit. Crypto tech provider **Haruko** disclosed a cyberattack affecting 15 clients, with some funds lost. When the vendor holding the keys gets breached, the tenant's audit history is irrelevant.

On the state-financing side, **OFAC** sanctioned the Iranian exchange **BitBank**, which Treasury says processed "Hormuz Safe" toll payments and helped route hundreds of millions in Bitcoin to the **IRGC** over roughly two months. Separately, **TRM** traced **274.60 ETH** to six operator addresses in a campaign where fake AI trading-bot tutorials tricked **224 victims** into deploying and funding their own drainer contracts. The best social engineering makes the mark do the signing.

Elsewhere, image host **Gyazo** confirmed 23.6 million user records stolen via a server flaw, and a **Hong Kong** banker drew four years for a $1.6B false-credit scheme that included **$470,000** in crypto bribes. Old crimes, new rails.

## Vulnerabilities Worth Your Attention

- **The full-chain iOS exploit.** **SlowMist**'s 23pds warned of an operationalized attack chain spanning iOS 13 through 26.5: a malicious Safari page triggers WebKit/JSC memory corruption, bypasses **PAC**, escapes the WebContent sandbox, escalates to root, and drains Keychains and wallet app data. Update your phone; a seed phrase on a stale iOS build is a bearer bond left on a park bench.
- **npm keeps rotting from the inside.** The **indexed-btree** package hides malicious code in normal runtime behavior to evade install-script defenses, while **WeaselBiscuit** shipped across 13 npm packages with functional overlap to DPRK's **Contagious Interview** tooling (**BeaverTail**, and kin). Install-time scanning was always going to lose to run-time payloads.
- **AI coding agents are the new soft underbelly.** **Plugin4Shell** lets a repo owner swap pinned plugin code across four AI agents (patched in **Claude Code 2.1.179** and **Codex 0.146.0**); **BragJack** hijacks AI browser assistants in **Chrome**, **Edge**, **Comet**, and **Claude in Chrome** via one malicious extension, earning two CVEs and over $20,000 in bounties.
- **Maximum severity, minimum drama.** **Microsoft** patched a CVSS 10.0 auth-bypass in **Azure AI Foundry** (**CVE-2026-85889**). **Orkes Conductor** is under active exploitation via an unauthenticated RCE (**CVE-2026-58138**, CVSS 9.8), and **CISA** added three actively exploited **Linux kernel** flaws to its KEV catalog. Patch the boring things first.
- **The CrowdSec tail.** May's **TanStack** npm compromise let an attacker copy roughly 170 private **CrowdSec** GitHub repos through a departed employee's still-open access. Offboarding is a security control.

## Threat Actors & Campaigns

**DPRK** remains the main character. Beyond **WaterPlum**'s $10.7M, **Chainalysis** reports blockchain dead-drop attacks up 420%, with North Korean operators using **Tron**, **Aptos**, and **BNB Smart Chain** as redundant C2 paths, and suspected **Iranian** actors encoding routing data in Bitcoin transactions. **WeaselBiscuit**'s Contagious Interview lineage rounds out the picture: they are recruiting developers, poisoning packages, and owning devices in parallel.

Others filled the margins. **PhantomRaven**, an npm infostealer, was likely LLM-authored per token-analysis patterns, evidence that the drainer economy now ships with a code assistant. **Transparent Tribe** (**APT36**) deployed new Rust backdoors against India and Afghanistan using private GitHub repos for C2, and China-linked **RatHat** abuses **ADB** to keep shell access after uninstall. Meanwhile **IOG** warned users off its own hijacked **YouTube** channel running an AI-manipulated **Hoskinson** doubling scam. The giveaway grift never dies; it just deepfakes better.

## The Bigger Picture

Washington chose the side door. After the **CLARITY Act** died in the Senate, the **CFTC** filed "Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions and Regulation Crypto Asset Markets" (RIN 3038-AF80) with the White House at prerule stage, and the **SEC** issued a five-year conditional "innovation exemption" letting tokenized **NMS** stocks trade on permissioned AMMs. Crypto stocks that sold off on the CLARITY collapse rallied within a day. The rules are coming from the agencies, not Congress, and the market already repriced that reality.

The tokenized-stock and perps land grab is on in earnest. **Coinbase**, **Kalshi**, and **Bitnomial** all filed for US single-stock perpetual futures; **Morpho** opened USDC borrowing against Coinbase's tokenized stock tokens on **Base**; and **S&P Global** is acquiring **OpenZeppelin**, which tells you where the audit money thinks the next decade lives. **Bastion** won conditional **OCC** trust-bank approval for stablecoin custody, and in the EU, banks now make up 23% of the **MiCA** register even as reports claim **Lagarde** personally intervened against **Binance**'s license.

Markets, as ever, were context. Bitcoin reclaimed $80,000 and touched $81,000, with **Glassnode** and **Bybit** noting short positions supplied 89% of every liquidated dollar in August's 24.6% run. Corporate treasuries bought just 5,900 BTC in three months, so this rally is squeeze mechanics, not fresh conviction. **Grayscale**'s **Zcash** ETF filed a 3-for-1 split after $233M of inflows, the privacy trade being the one genuinely new thing on the board.
